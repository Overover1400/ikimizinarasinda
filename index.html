English

İkimizin Arasında (“Between the Two of Us”) is a Turkish-language YouTube channel making plain-spoken personal-development and relationship-psychology videos based on research: attachment, self-worth, ego and the patterns we repeat. Sources are named on screen. The narrator's Turkish is not perfect, and that honesty is part of the channel.

Channel: youtube.com/@ikimizin.arasinda · Contact: tisheh@gmail.com

About this site and the app

This page is the official home page of the channel and of a small desktop tool called “İkimizin Arasında Uploader”. The tool is used by one person only, the owner of the channel, to upload and schedule the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API, and to manage comments on the owner's own videos. It is not distributed to the public and never accesses any other user's data.
